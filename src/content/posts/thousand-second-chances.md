We often talk about second chances as if they come once in a blue moon. But the truth is, every morning is a quiet second chance.

## Borrowed Time

When you realize that every single day is extra credit, the pressure shifts. You stop trying to perform perfection and start trying to practice presence.

> "Movement, even awkward, limping, reluctant movement, is the only real sign that you’re not stuck."
