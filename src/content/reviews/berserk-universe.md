In this world, is the destiny of mankind controlled by some transcendental entity or law? Is it like the hand of God hovering above? At least it is true that man has no control, even over his own will.

## Guts: The Struggle Against Causality

Guts is the ultimate struggler. In a world governed by cosmic entities and dark sacrifices, Guts refuses to bow to fate. Armed with a slab of iron too big to be called a sword, he fights simply to endure.

Kentaro Miura's artwork is legendary, with cross-hatching detail that captures both gothic terror and deep tenderness.
