Anyone can wear the mask. You can wear the mask. If you didn't know that, now you do.

## The Burden of the Mask

What separates Spider-Man from almost every other superhero in pop culture is that Peter Parker (and Miles Morales) is constantly punished for doing the right thing. Being Spider-Man ruins your personal life, destroys your relationships, and leaves you broke.

Yet, he gets back up. Every single time.

## A Leap of Faith

Miles' story isn't about becoming Peter Parker. It's about finding your own rhythm in a universe that insists on telling you who you ought to be.

The iconic leap of faith scene, accompanied by Daniel Pemberton's score and 'Sunflower', remains one of the greatest moments in animation history.
