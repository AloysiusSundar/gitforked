Who am I? You sure you want to know? The story of my life is not for the faint of heart. If somebody told you that it was a run of the mill tale, if somebody told you that I'm just your average burnt out engineering graduate trying to find his own footing in this wretched world... they'd be right. That uh.. sounds about accurate.

\
But let me assure you: This, like any story worth telling, is all about a nobody with an unhealthy obsession of becoming a somebody.

\
Hello there, The name's Aloy. Sometimes I go by Vulture, Skip or Lammy depending on who you ask. I'm an engineering undergrad with a painstakingly droning (and surprisingly draining) life. Join me as I try to cope with my absolute shitshow of a life through shitposting and more, trying to make the most of all the time that I’ve borrowed.

\
I started this to not only showcase what I can do but also to try and find my passion for writing again. It is also a space for me to share with you, the reader about what interests me most, to give you a glimpse into my thoughts as I traverse through life, trying to find my place in this beautiful and equally cruel world.
